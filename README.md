# Split Decision

**Two writers, one couch, zero decisions.**

A small web app for people who've finished **Split Fiction** and want to jump back in without deciding where. It picks a random level or side story to replay from the game's chapter select.

## Features

- **Random pick**: deals a random entry from 72 levels and 12 side stories across all 8 chapters. A fresh pick is dealt on every visit, and your last 5 picks are skipped.
- **Filters**:
  - Whose story it is (Mio's sci-fi, Zoe's fantasy, or both)
  - Energy (Chill / Medium / Intense)
  - Type (level or side story)
  - Boss fights (any, only bosses, no bosses)

  Tapping **Done** deals a new pick with the chosen filters.
- **Level cards**: an image, the chapter, tags, a tagline and a short description of what happens in the level (spoilers included).
- **Remembers your filters**: they're stored in the browser, per device. No account or backend.
